# Code of Conduct

Clippr is built by people with different backgrounds, skills and levels of experience. We want
taking part to be a good experience for everyone.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and assume good faith.
- Give and accept constructive feedback about work, not about people.
- Be patient with newcomers; everyone was a beginner once.
- Credit others' work.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory language or imagery.
- Personal attacks, trolling, or sustained disruption.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Sexualised content or unwelcome attention.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions and chat — and when
representing the project elsewhere.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report problems privately to the maintainers (see the repository's contact details). Reports
are handled confidentially. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and temporarily or
permanently ban participants who break this code.

This code is inspired by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
